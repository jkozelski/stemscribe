## Executive summary

Swap the chord detector from librosa+24-template to **Chord-CNN-LSTM (Jiang ISMIR 2019, 301 classes including slash chords)**, run in-process on Hetzner, and **turn the Anthropic corrector OFF on the Jiang path**. Today's 13-song UG-benchmark measurement: librosa+V1 corrector = **root F1 0.712 / quality 0.263 / slash 0**; Jiang raw = **root F1 0.809 / quality 0.554 / slash 69 across 11 songs**. The corrector is a no-op on Jiang (qflip safety gate refuses Claude's edits on 11/13 songs) and *destroys* slash chords on the 2 songs where it does fire (V1 prompt strips `/bass` by design). The detector swap delivers +0.097 root F1, +0.291 quality F1, and the first slash-chord support from audio in StemScriber history — in a single configuration change. Soft-launch target: ship the swap by June 5 with a one-week prod soak, hold a Jun 12 decision gate at root F1 ≥ 0.80, fall back to current librosa+V1 only if the soak surfaces a regression bigger than the Iron Man scorer mismatch we already understand.

### Killed hypotheses (negative results that simplify the plan)

- **Tuning compensation in the librosa path** (`librosa.estimate_tuning` + `pitch_shift`, the Mar 9 root-cause doc's #1 recommendation). Agent B measured +0.0005 root F1 raw and **−0.036 with the corrector** — Dirty Work cratered 0.677 → 0.139 because pitch_shift artifacts corrupt the chord_set Claude reasons over. Drop. Detail in `docs/v3-agent-B-tuning-compensation-2026-05-13.md`.
- **Slash-chord disambiguation via Jiang + bass-stem hybrid.** Agent C built `upgrade_to_slash(jiang_chord, bass_pc)` and tested two variants: permissive (3rd/5th/7th in bass) gained 1 TP and added 22 FPs (−0.107 full F1); strict (3rd or 7th only) gained 0 TPs and added 9 FPs. UG ground truth doesn't notate the inversions bar-quantized bass detection finds. The right experiment is sub-beat bass tracking targeted at descending-bass cliches (Petty IGWO, Hotel California intro) — post-launch. Detail in `docs/v3-agent-C-slash-hybrid-2026-05-13.md`.

### What we keep

- **In-process Jiang on Hetzner CPX41** — Agent D measured 7.83 s wall on M3 Max, estimated 10–15 s on CPX41 vCPU. 27 MB checkpoints on disk, ~500 MB RSS × 4 concurrent jobs = 2 GB peak, fits 16 GB comfortably. Modal A10G would save 4–10 s but adds 5–10 s cold-start round-trip — net wash with more failure modes. Revisit only if Jiang sustains 25+ s under concurrency. Detail in `docs/v3-agent-D-cleanup-deploy-2026-05-13.md`.
- **The corrector code stays in the repo**, gated by `ENABLE_ANTHROPIC_CORRECTION`. Keep it as the librosa-path companion so the fallback (`USE_JIANG_DETECTOR=false`) preserves today's 0.71 number rather than regressing to raw librosa's 0.44.

## Risks not handled by the plan above

1. **Anthropic corrector OFF means no recovery layer.** When librosa+V1 misidentified a chord, Claude could fix it. With Jiang+nothing, what Jiang emits is what the user sees. **Mitigation:** `📋 My Chart` paste is the explicit escape hatch (already shipped). Plus the post-launch reranker work re-introduces a Claude layer in a non-destructive shape (top-K pick, not wholesale rewrite).
2. **Jiang memory under sustained 4-way concurrency unverified on CPX41.** Agent D estimated 2 GB peak; not measured. **Mitigation:** Week 1 smoke test + Week 2 active monitoring. Roll back if RSS climbs unbounded.
3. **Iron Man (power-chord rock) is genuinely worse on Jiang's root F1.** Even after the scorer fix, the underlying Jiang behavior on distorted-guitar power-chord riffs may be worse than librosa's. **Mitigation:** if the Week 2 diagnosis shows Jiang is the actual problem (not the scorer), accept it given the +0.097 average lift and add Iron Man to the "songs where `📋 My Chart` is the recommended workflow" list in the launch FAQ.
4. **The 13-song cohort is rock/jazz heavy.** No country (Garth Brooks GT exists but no local audio), no early-2000s pop, no R&B beyond Stevie Wonder, no metal beyond Iron Man. The +0.097 average may not generalize. **Mitigation:** Week 3 benchmark expansion to all 38 fixtures with audio. If the broader benchmark drops below 0.75 average, that's a hold-and-reassess signal at the Jun 5 gate.
