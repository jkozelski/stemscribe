## 3. The aesthetic direction (lead with this)

**Anchor concept: "Tear The Sound Apart" + 80s artifact aesthetic.** The product literally splits sound; the brand should feel cinematic and slightly nostalgic. Per your earlier framing: Kevin brings 80s beauty-commercial energy. That's a real brand asset — most music tools look like generic SaaS. Lean into it.

**Visual moves to explore:**
- Soft warm rim lighting on instruments and hands
- Tape grain / VHS artifacts (subtle — not gimmicky)
- Slow-mo close-ups: pick scraping a string, fingers on a fretboard, a needle on vinyl
- Isolated colors against deep black: coral/orange on near-black (matches site)
- Long held shots, less cutting, more atmosphere

**What we're NOT:** AI hype, neon-cyberpunk, generic "tech startup" aesthetic. We're a music tool, not a Silicon Valley app.

## 4. First concrete deliverable — pick ONE for Kevin to commit to

Three options, ranked by effort:

**A. The 30-second teaser ("hero film")** — *highest impact*
A single ~30s film for landing-page hero + every social channel. Footage idea: slow-mo of a record literally being separated — physical metaphor for what the product does. Or: musician's hands fail at chord progression on a printed bad-tab, hand reaches for laptop with StemScriber, chord chart appears, hand returns to fretboard, plays clean. Could be wordless, music-driven. **Two weeks to shoot + cut.**

**B. The "Frustration → Relief" reel (9:16 for IG/TikTok)** — *medium effort*
15-second cut. First 7s: musician fighting a bad Ultimate Guitar tab, missing the chord, bad sound. Last 8s: same musician with StemScriber pulled up, hits the chord clean. Title card: "Stop guessing. Start playing." or your "We don't guess. We listen." line. **One week to shoot + cut.**

**C. Founder POV ("Why I built this")** — *lower effort, founder-led*
You + guitar in your home studio, talking to camera 60-90s about why you started this. The "I'm tired of bad chord charts" rant. Authentic, not glossy. Kevin shoots/lights/edits — you provide the words. **One day to shoot, week to edit.**

**My pick to push for:** **A**, because the hero film becomes the landing-page anchor (closes the audit's "abstract waveform hero" gap) AND drops on every social channel as the launch teaser. One asset, max leverage. If Kevin's bandwidth says no, fall back to B.
