3. **Lyric-chord alignment fix** — Jeff's "chord over the wrong word" complaint. INVESTIGATED 2026-05-21, ready for a targeted fix:
   - **Bug location:** `chart_formatter.py` → `_place_chords_on_words()` (line ~1950).
   - **Root cause:** for each chord it picks the word with the minimum `abs(word.start - chord.time)` — pure nearest-neighbor by time. Two problems: (a) the chord's `time` is the BAR downbeat (`b["start_time"]`), but singers phrase behind/ahead of the beat, so the temporally-nearest word is often NOT the word being sung when the chord lands; (b) `abs()` has no left/right bias — a word 200ms *before* the downbeat beats a word 200ms *after*, but musically the chord sits above the word sung *at or just after* the change.
   - **Suggested fix:** prefer the first word whose `start >= chord.time` (the word sung when/after the chord lands), fall back to nearest only if no word follows in the line. Small change, contained to one function.
   - **Verify:** the function has no dedicated test — add one, and eyeball a real song's chart. ~1 hr including the fix. Stage + deploy.
