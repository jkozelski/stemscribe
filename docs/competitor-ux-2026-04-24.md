# Competitor UX + Positioning Report — 2026-04-24

Scope: how Chordify, Moises, Ultimate Guitar, Songsterr, Klangio, and Hooktheory PRESENT chord charts and what they pitch as their value. Engineering is out of scope.

## 2. Per-company detail

### Chordify (chordify.net)
- **Layout:** beat grid — each beat is a square, chord labels on the first beat of each new chord; vertical lines mark bars; dark cursor square marches left-to-right with playback. Views: Animated, Summary, Chords & Lyrics. Right-side Diagrams tab = fingering for guitar/uke/piano/mandolin.
- **Practice:** transpose, capo, BPM slow-down, loop, MIDI download, PDF export, setlists, offline — all Premium.
- **Mobile:** portrait vertical grid, tap-to-seek, auto-scroll.
- **Key/tempo:** top-of-chart.
- **Export:** PDF + print (Premium).
- **Headline:** "Learn and play all your favorite songs." Tag: "Tune Into Chords."
- **Target:** casual hobbyist — guitar/uke/piano/mandolin.

### Moises (moises.ai)
- **Layout:** horizontal auto-scrolling chord strip; current chord highlighted, chord diagram inline. Three views (Easy/Medium/Advanced); Advanced surfaces slash chords, 7ths, m7b5, aug7.
- **Practice:** stem mixer is the headliner (mute/solo/volume), plus speed, pitch/key shift, metronome, loop, seek. Lyrics on a separate scrolling track.
- **Mobile:** flagship; portrait + landscape.
- **Key/tempo:** in player header w/ time signature.
- **Export:** no PDF chord-sheet UX.
- **Headline:** "The Creative Suite for Musicians." Sub: "essential toolkit for musicians to practice, perform, create and collaborate anywhere."
- **Target:** Drummers/Vocalists/Bassists/Guitarists/Producers/Educators. Berklee + "Grammy-winning artists" proof.

### Ultimate Guitar (ultimate-guitar.com)
- **Layout:** two products. (a) Community **Chords** sheets — plain-text chord-over-lyrics with `[Verse 1]`/`[Pre-Chorus]`/`[Chorus]`/`[Bridge]` brackets (ChordPro-ish). (b) **Tab Player** (Pro) — rendered staff + TAB with playback cursor and per-instrument tracks. No beat-grid.
- **Practice:** transpose, SmartScroll (mobile mic follows your playing), tempo, loop, simplify-chords, AI coach (mobile), multi-track mute/solo with backing tracks on 4,000+ Official tabs, autoscroll.
- **Mobile:** app-first. Chord sheets portrait; Tab Player landscape.
- **Key/tempo:** top of tab; persistent transpose pill.
- **Export:** print to PDF.
- **Headline:** "world's largest catalog" — 2M+ tabs / 800k+ chord sheets.
- **Pricing:** Free (ads); Pro ~$39.99/yr promo → $99.99/yr list, or ~$24.99/mo. 100-tab offline cap.
- **Target:** hobbyist → intermediate guitar/bass/uke.

### Songsterr (songsterr.com)
- **Layout:** rendered staff + TAB, not a chord chart. Chord symbols appear above staff where relevant; primary object is note-level score.
- **Practice:** note-highlight auto-scroll (note lights ~120 ms before sounding), pitch-preserved speed, loop measures, solo/mute per-track, count-in, tuner, fullscreen — all Plus.
- **Mobile:** portrait multiline tab; landscape full player.
- **Key/tempo:** score header.
- **Export:** print, MIDI, Guitar Pro — all Plus.
- **Headline:** "Guitar Tabs with Rhythm." Sub: "Pause-free sync with original audio."
- **Pricing:** Free = MIDI only; Plus $9.99/mo.
- **Target:** guitar/bass/drums learning specific parts, rock/metal/alt.

### Klangio (klang.io)
- **Layout:** engraved sheet music, guitar TAB, piano roll. Vocals with lyrics under staff. Chord symbols lead-sheet-style. Not a practice player — the artifact IS the chart.
- **Practice:** Edit Mode for fixing notes. No playback cursor, loop, or tempo/key change for practice. Output → MuseScore/Logic/Ableton/Guitar Pro.
- **Mobile:** desktop/web-first (MuseHub).
- **Key/tempo:** score header.
- **Export:** PDF, MIDI (quantized + unquantized), MusicXML, Guitar Pro, LilyPond.
- **Headline:** "AI Music Transcription — Convert Audio to Sheet Music." Sub: "From Music, MP3 and YouTube to Sheet Music, TABs, MIDI, and MusicXML."
- **Pricing:** 20s free preview; Studio Pro ~$11.99/mo annual, ~$29.99/mo monthly; per-app "ticket" tiers (50 or 250/mo).
- **Target:** teachers/composers/transcribers/producers.

### Hooktheory (hooktheory.com)
- **Layout:** colored block grid labeled Roman numerals (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°); each scale degree gets a distinct color (I red → vii° pink). Absolute names (C, dm…) as secondary label under each block. Melody on piano-roll staff above chords.
- **Practice:** Hookpad — playback, tempo, key change, drag-to-edit, Aria AI chord/melody generation. TheoryTab: 65,000-song database with playback + lesson panel linking progression to theory.
- **Mobile:** web-based, not mobile-first.
- **Key/tempo:** top-of-song header; changing key re-colors the chart instantly.
- **Export:** Hookpad exports MIDI/MusicXML.
- **Headline:** "Smart songwriting starts here."
- **Pricing:** Free tier; $4.99/mo or $49/yr.
- **Target:** songwriters/producers/students/teachers.

## 3. Gaps and differentiation (research observations)

**Standard across this market (what "everyone else does"):**
- **Playback-synced auto-scroll / moving cursor on the chord chart.** Chordify (beat cursor), Moises (scrolling strip with highlighted current chord), UG Tab Player, Songsterr, Hookpad all do this. Klangio is the only subject that omits it — and it isn't a practice tool.
- **Tempo slow-down without pitch change** — Chordify, Moises, UG Pro, Songsterr Plus.
- **Transpose + capo hints** surfaced on the chord chart — Chordify, UG, Moises.
- **Per-instrument mute/solo** tied to the chord chart — Moises (stem split), UG Official tabs, Songsterr (pre-recorded stems).
- **Tap/click-to-seek on a chord or beat** — Chordify, Moises, Songsterr, Hookpad.
- **Multiple complexity levels for the same song** — Moises (Easy/Medium/Advanced) and UG ("simplify chords") both ship this independently.
- **Chord diagrams (fingering popovers) inline with the current chord** — Chordify and Moises show the guitar/uke/piano shape without navigating away.
- **Section labels** (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, etc.) — standard everywhere a chord sheet is rendered; UG made the bracket format canonical.
- **PDF export / printable chord sheet** gated behind paid — Chordify, UG, Songsterr, Klangio all offer it; Moises is the outlier.
- **Landing-page claim of a specific number** — UG "2M+ tabs," Hooktheory "65,000 songs," Songsterr "400,000 songs / 60,000 artists." Scale-as-pitch is universal.

**Unique to individual competitors (observations only):**
- **Chordify:** beat-grid primitive (every beat = one square) is specific to Chordify; nobody else renders charts this way.
- **Moises:** pairs the chord chart with a stem mixer on the same screen — the stems-plus-chords combo is Moises's signature.
- **Hookpad:** colors chord blocks by scale-degree function and labels Roman numerals by default — theory-forward rendering nobody else uses.
- **Ultimate Guitar:** SmartScroll (mobile mic listens and scrolls with the player) and a separate AI practice coach.
- **Songsterr:** pre-playback note illumination (~120 ms lead) on note-level tab rendering.
- **Klangio:** six export formats (PDF, MIDI quantized/unquantized, MusicXML, Guitar Pro, LilyPond) — deepest export matrix of the set.

**Target-user segmentation cues:**
- Chordify + UG free tier = hobbyist/casual.
- Moises = "musician" (broad, multi-instrument, mobile-first).
- Hooktheory = songwriter/student (theory-forward).
- Klangio = transcriber/educator/producer (output for downstream DAW).
- Songsterr = player learning a specific part (rock/metal/alt).
