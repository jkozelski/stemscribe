How It Works

Three Steps. Any Song.

From a recording to a playable chord chart in minutes — auto-built from the audio, editable when you want it exact.

MP3 1
Drop your track
Any audio file — MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A. Drag it in. That's the whole setup.
2
We split & chart it
6 isolated stems plus an editable, bar-aligned chord chart — auto-detected, or paste your own from Ultimate Guitar.
3
Play & practice
Mix stems, slow it down, loop tricky parts, and the chart auto-scrolls while you play.
