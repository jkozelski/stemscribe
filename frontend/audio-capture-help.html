Got the file?

If you have the audio, upload it. If you don't have the audio, this page tells you how musicians actually get it. Read this first — the rule at the bottom matters.

The clean way

Most of the time you already have the file and don't realize it. Try these before anything else.

  • Bandcamp Re-download anything you've bought from your collection page. Best quality, fully yours.
  • iTunes / Music app Right-click any track → Show in Finder. The file is sitting on your computer.
  • Amazon Music Open the app, My Music, hit Download.
  • Internet Archive Free, public-domain, or artist-permissioned recordings. Paste the URL into StemScriber directly.
  • Your own DAW Bounce your demo, your livestream archive, your jam recording.

The DIY way

If the audio is something you have the right to use but it's stuck inside a video or a stream you can play on your computer or phone, you can record it yourself. The tools below are widely used by archivists, podcasters, and musicians for legitimate work.

  • Mac QuickTime Player + BlackHole (both free) records system audio to .m4a. Audio Hijack (~$70) is the no-fuss option if you do this often.
  • Windows OBS Studio (free) records desktop audio. Audacity (free) does the same with WASAPI loopback.
  • iPhone Built-in screen recorder captures whatever's playing. Save the .mov, drop it on StemScriber — we extract the audio for you.
  • Android Same idea — built-in screen recorder, enable internal audio.
  • Power users yt-dlp -x --audio-format mp3 <URL> on your own machine. It's free. We don't run it for you, but you can run it for yourself.

The rule

"Whatever you upload, you're saying you have the right to use it.
That part doesn't change because the file came from somewhere convenient." — StemScriber Terms §4.3 + §4.4, in plain English

Audio you own, audio licensed to you, or audio in the public domain — you're set. For everything else, the rights question travels with the file: whoever uploads it is the one who needs the right to use it. We're the tool that splits and charts what you bring.
